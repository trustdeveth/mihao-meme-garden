Overview

Mihao Meme Garden is a multiplayer 3D garden game in a blocky Roblox style, built around the Mihao cat meme. It runs in your web browser at mihaomeme.garden.

Up to 12 players share a village. Each has a private garden plot around a central market. You can farm, sell and dress up without a wallet. Pet eggs and shop decorations are designed to cost MGRD, so they cannot be bought while token features are off. Some decorations can be earned by playing.

Gameplay

Grow crops, sell them, and spend the coins on a bigger and better garden.

  1. Buy seeds from Mo the Mihao.
  2. Plant them anywhere on your unlocked soil.
  3. Water them. They keep growing while you are away.
  4. Harvest them. Crops at peak ripeness sell for more.
  5. Sell them to Gus the Mihao at the market.
  6. Buy new seeds, bigger soil, homes, gadgets and outfits.

Meme features

Every player is a Mihao: a serious ginger cat with white brows and a long mustache. These features are for fun. They never give coins, gems, Season Points or MGRD.

  • Wardrobe: hats, tops, bottoms, shoes, 6 mustache styles and cat fur colors.
  • Poses and speech bubbles: 6 poses and 8 lines. Meme Points, up to 60 a day, only unlock poses.
  • Village fun: meme moments, Official Inspection, Beard Battle, Pointing Contest and garden stamps.
  • Cameras: photos, short clips and an optional instant replay.
  • Weekly Meme Challenge: send a link to your post. A moderator approves what others see. There are no coin or token prizes.

Tokenomics

MGRD is designed as an optional token for weekly rewards and in-game purchases. The plan is to launch it on Solana through Pump.fun. It has not launched. The game's Solana program has been tested on a private test network, not on a public one.

Coins, gems, Season Points and Meme Points stay inside the game. They cannot be exchanged for MGRD.

Supply and reward funding

Pump.fun sells the token to the public on its bonding curve. There is no private sale and no tokens set aside before launch. Any tokens the team holds are bought on the market, and the team plans to fund player rewards from what it buys. The amount the team will buy has not been announced.

The game does not force the team to add funds. A weekly pool exists only after tokens are in the rewards vault and the week is published. A week without funding would offer game-only prizes. The plan limits first-year player rewards to 6,000,000 MGRD, and at most 500,000 in a month. That limit is a team policy, not something the program enforces.

Weekly reward plan

Rewards come from playing. A funded weekly pool is shared by Season Points. Buying things or holding tokens adds no points. Pets and the paid Grow All can still help you harvest more during festivals, which earns festival points.

That is at most 80 points on Monday to Thursday and 100 on a festival day: 620 points in a full week.

The weekly steps

  1. Play. A week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC.
  2. Link a Solana wallet (Menu, then MGRD Rewards). Signing the link message costs nothing. Link it before the week's results are prepared: players without a linked wallet are left out of that week.
  3. Results. After the week ends, the team prepares the list and publishes it on Solana. The target is Wednesday, after 48 hours of checks. This is done by people, so it can be late.
  4. Claim from your wallet within the claim period, normally 30 days. Each week can be claimed once. You pay the Solana network fee and a small SOL deposit for the accounts the claim creates.

How your share is worked out

  • Your share: weekly pool × your points ÷ the points of all eligible players.
  • Limit: one player gets at most 1% of a weekly pool.
  • Small amounts: less than 1 MGRD is carried to a later week. A carried amount is added on top of the limit.
  • Weekly pool: the planned setting is 100,000 MGRD. The team can set a different pool for a week.
  • Not claimed or not shared: it stays in the rewards vault. The team can move vault tokens that are not set aside for an open week.

Where shop payments go

With the default settings, half of each shop payment goes to the rewards vault and half to the team's treasury. Tips go straight to the other player.

Token burns

The plan ties burning to real use of the game:

  • 20% of all MGRD paid in the shop is set aside to burn. Shop purchases are paid in MGRD, so these tokens do not need to be bought first. With the default split this is 20 of the 50 MGRD that reach the treasury from each 100 MGRD payment.
  • The team burns by hand. It is not automatic. Burning removes tokens from the supply for good.
  • Burns are shown in the game under MGRD Rewards, then Community, with the amount burned, the amount still waiting and a link to each transaction. The record is entered by the team, so check the transaction links.

Using part of other income, such as Pump.fun creator fees, to buy MGRD and burn it is being considered. Nothing has been decided. Burning does not promise any price change.

For Mihao meme holders (planned)

The game is built around the Mihao meme, so the team plans special thank-you perks for people who hold the Mihao meme token. This is not built yet. The exact token address, the amounts needed and the perks will be announced first.

  • The ideas are cosmetic: a holder badge, a special name colour, an exclusive Mihao outfit and early access to limited drops.
  • Holding would not add Season Points or increase rewards.
  • The game would only read the balance of a wallet you link with a signature. You would never need to send tokens to anyone.

What the team can change

The team's accounts for the game's Solana program can:

  • connect the token to the program, which works only once and only with the wallet that deployed it;
  • update the program's code, as long as it stays upgradeable, which can change any rule on this page;
  • change the share of shop payments that goes to the rewards vault, anywhere from 0% to 100%;
  • change the treasury account;
  • pause claims, shop payments and new Garden Club locks (unlocking still works);
  • publish each week's reward list, and so choose who is on it;
  • move vault tokens that are not set aside for an open reward week;
  • hand the admin role to another account, which has to accept it.

With the current code, the admin cannot take Garden Club locks or withdraw tokens set aside for an open reward week. Because the code can be updated, you are also trusting whoever holds the update right. The team has not yet decided how to protect it. The program has not had an independent security review.

Risks and open points

  • Nothing is final. The token name, prices, rewards, perks and launch can all change, or the token may never launch.
  • No value is promised. A crypto token can lose all of its value, and there may be no buyers. New tokens on launch platforms are especially risky.
  • Trading fees. Pump.fun's fees apply when you buy or sell. Check them before you trade.
  • Software can fail. Do not treat the program as audited or risk-free. It has not had an independent security review.
  • Paid random items. Pet eggs are random. Rules for paid random items differ by country and age.
  • Paid help. Pets, paid Grow All and larger soil may help a player earn more festival points.
  • Other payment methods. Card, SOL and stablecoin checkout are not available. SOL is only needed for network fees and account deposits.
  • Bridges. The planned BNB Chain bridge does not exist. Do not trust any bridged MGRD token before the team announces it here.
  • Beware of fakes. MGRD has not launched, so there is no official token address yet. Anyone can create a token with the same name. Be careful with any token or message that says you can buy it now. The team will never ask for your recovery phrase.
