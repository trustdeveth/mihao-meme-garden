Overview

Mihao Meme Garden is a multiplayer 3D garden game in a blocky Roblox style, built around the Mihao cat meme. It runs in your web browser at mihaomeme.garden.

Up to 12 players share a village. Each has a private garden plot around a central market. You can farm, sell and dress up without a wallet. Pet eggs and shop decorations are designed to cost MGRD, so they cannot be bought while token features are off. Some decorations can be earned by playing.

Gameplay

Grow crops, sell them, and spend the coins on a bigger and better garden.

  1. Buy seeds from Mo the Mihao.
  2. Plant them anywhere on your unlocked soil.
  3. Water them. They keep growing while you are away.
  4. Harvest them. Crops at peak ripeness sell for more.
  5. Sell them to Gus the Mihao at the market.
  6. Buy new seeds, bigger soil, homes, gadgets and outfits.

Meme features

Every player is a Mihao: a serious ginger cat with white brows and a long mustache. These features are for fun. They never give coins, gems, Season Points or MGRD.

  • Wardrobe: hats, tops, bottoms, shoes, 6 mustache styles and cat fur colors.
  • Poses and speech bubbles: 6 poses and 8 lines. Meme Points, up to 60 a day, only unlock poses.
  • Village fun: meme moments, Official Inspection, Beard Battle, Pointing Contest and garden stamps.
  • Cameras: photos, short clips and an optional instant replay.
  • Weekly Meme Challenge: send a link to your post. A moderator approves what others see. There are no coin or token prizes.

Tokenomics

MGRD is designed as an optional token for weekly rewards and in-game purchases. The plan is to launch it on Flap, on BNB Smart Chain. It has not launched. The game's rewards contract has been tested on a private test chain.

Coins, gems, Season Points and Meme Points stay inside the game. They cannot be exchanged for MGRD.

Supply and reward funding

Flap sells the token to the public as it launches. The team plans to fund player rewards with the tokens it buys at launch. The amount has not been decided.

An earlier draft split the supply by percentage between community, team, treasury and others. That split does not apply to this launch.

The rewards contract does not force the team to add funds. A weekly pool exists only after tokens are in the contract and the week is published. A week without funding would offer game-only prizes. The proposal still limits first-year community rewards to 6,000,000 MGRD, and at most 500,000 in a month.

Weekly rewards

A funded weekly pool is shared by Season Points, up to 100 a day. Purchases and token holdings add no points directly. However, pets and paid Grow All can add festival points through the harvests they help with.

  • Pool: the current default setting is 100,000 MGRD. A pool exists only when it is funded and published.

Example only: with one weekend festival, a player can reach about 620 points in a week. If all eligible players total 350,000 points and the pool is 100,000 MGRD, that player's share is about 177 MGRD. This is not a promise of any amount or value.

Spending MGRD

Prices are fixed amounts of MGRD and do not follow a market price. They can change. Paying or locking asks the wallet twice: first to allow that exact amount, then to confirm. Each step needs a little BNB for the network fee. A payment sent after its price quote has expired is refused, so no MGRD is taken.

Where shop payments go

With the default settings, half of each shop payment goes to the rewards vault and half to the team's treasury. Tips go straight to the other player.

What the team can change

In the current design, the owner account of the rewards contract, held by the team, can:

  • connect the token to the rewards contract, which works only once;
  • change the share of shop payments that goes to the rewards vault, anywhere from 0% to 100%;
  • change the treasury account;
  • pause claims, shop payments and new Garden Club locks (unlocking still works);
  • publish each week's reward list;
  • move vault tokens that are not reserved for an open reward week and are not locked in the Garden Club;
  • take out BNB or other tokens (not MGRD) that reach the contract, including holder dividends;
  • hand the owner role to another account, which has to accept it.

The owner cannot take Garden Club locks, withdraw tokens reserved for an open reward week, or swap the connected token for another. The rewards contract cannot be upgraded, so these rules cannot be changed after deployment. The owner does choose who is on each week's reward list. The contract has not had an independent security review.

Holder dividends. It has not been decided whether the token pays them. If it does, the rewards contract earns them as a holder, including on tokens locked in the Garden Club. Dividends paid in MGRD join the rewards vault. Dividends paid in anything else can be taken out by the team. Players do not receive them for locked tokens.

Risks and open points

  • Nothing is final. The token name, allocation, prices, rewards and launch can all change, or the token may never launch.
  • No value is promised. A crypto token can lose all of its value, and there may be no buyers.
  • Trading tax. With a tax token, part of each buy and sell is taken as tax. Check the rates before you trade.
  • Software can fail. Do not treat the contracts as audited or risk-free. The rewards contract cannot be upgraded, so a mistake in it cannot be patched in place.
  • Paid random items. Pet eggs are random. Rules for paid random items differ by country and age.
  • Paid help. Pets, paid Grow All and larger soil may help a player earn more festival points.
  • Other payment methods. Card, BNB and stablecoin checkout are not available. BNB is only needed for network fees.
  • Beware of fakes. MGRD has not launched on any public network, so there is no official contract address yet. Be careful with any token or message that says you can buy it now.
